Renjie Ma

I obtained my B. Eng degree in Automation from Hefei University of Technology, Hefei, China, in 2016, and PhD degree in Control Science and Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, China, in 2022. I am currently an Associate Researcher (Associate Professor) of Mechanical Engineering at the State Key Laboratory of Robotics and Systems and the School of Mechatronics Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, China. My current research interests include optimization and control for complex systems and intelligent robots. I serve as a member of Technical Committee on Embodied Artificial Intelligence of Chinese Association of Automation and a member of Technical Committee on Data-Driven Control and Monitoring of IEEE Industrial Electronics Society.

Research

Intelligent Robotic Control

My research develops learning, optimization, and feedback-control methods with rigorous guarantees on stability, safety, robustness, and communication efficiency. Current interests span neural learning control, safe motion planning and control, data-driven and adaptive control, aperiodic-sampled control, and robotic systems.
